

# Healing App - Engagement & Monetization Improvements

This document outlines proposed features to increase user engagement, retention, and revenue for the healing app.

---

## 1. Progress Tracking

**Video Completion Tracking**
- Videos will be marked as "completed" when a user watches 90% or more
- Users can see which videos they've finished and pick up where they left off while reopning the app.

**Visual Progress Indicators**
- Home page will show a progress circle for the current course (e.g., "12/22 videos completed")
- Each course card will display a progress bar showing completion percentage
- Sections will show completion status (e.g., "3/5 videos done")

**Auto-Resume**
- When users open the app, it will automatically scroll to their next unwatched video
- A "Continue Learning" card at the top will highlight the next video to watch
- Videos will resume from the exact position where the user left off

---

## 2. Post-Video Experience

**Completion Dialog**
After finishing a video, users will see a dialog showing:
- Their progress in the current section
- Options to either close or continue to the next video

**Section Completion Celebration**
When a user completes all videos in a section, they'll see a celebration screen showing:
- Section name they just mastered
- Overall course progress
- Preview of the next section

**Course Completion Celebration**
When a user finishes an entire course, they'll see:
- Congratulations message
- Total videos watched and time spent
- Option to explore other courses

---

## 3. Stats Dashboard

A new "My Journey" page where users can view their learning statistics:

**Core Stats**
- Total watch time across all videos
- Number of videos completed
- Number of courses completed
- Number of sections completed
- Current course progress percentage

This gives users a sense of accomplishment and encourages continued engagement.

---

## 4. Monetization Improvements

**Interstitial Ads**
- Show interstitial ads when users tap "Next" ont the dialog to play the next video
- Frequency: Every 2-3 videos (not after every single video to avoid frustration)
- Always show interstitial ad after section completion and course completion
- Premium users will not see any ads

**Rewarded Ads for Premium Content**
- When a user taps on a locked/premium video, they'll have two options:
  1. "Upgrade to Premium" - purchase subscription
  2. "Watch Ad to Unlock" - watch a rewarded ad to access the video
- After watching the ad, that video becomes available for the rest of the day
- Limit: 2-3 rewarded unlocks per day to encourage premium upgrades

**Banner Ads**
- Keep existing banners on breathing timer
- Add banner to the stats dashboard page
- Small banner at bottom of video completion dialog

---

## 5. Re-engagement Notifications

**Inactive User Reminders**
- After 24 hours: "Continue your healing journey - you're 60% through Structure!"
- After 3 days: "Your body needs movement. Take 5 minutes for yourself today."
- After 7 days: "We miss you! Pick up where you left off in Tai Chi Fundamentals"
- Incomplete section reminder (48 hours): "Just 2 more videos to complete the Flexibility section!"

**Progress Celebrations**
- When user reaches 50% of a course: "Halfway there! You've mastered 50% of Tai Chi Fundamentals"
- When a new section is available: "New section unlocked: Power. Ready to continue?"

All notifications work offline using local scheduling - no server required.

---

## 6. UI/UX Improvements

**Home Page**
- Continue Watching card prominently showing the next video
- Progress ring displaying current course completion


**Video List**
- Checkmarks on completed videos
- Section dividers with completion status

---

## Summary

These improvements focus on:

1. **Progress Visibility** - Users always know where they are and how far they've come
2. **Completion Loops** - Smooth flow from one video to the next with celebration moments
3. **Balanced Monetization** - Strategic ad placements that generate revenue without disrupting the experience
4. **Gentle Re-engagement** - Notifications that encourage users to return without being annoying


The goal is to make users feel accomplished, keep them engaged longer, and provide multiple revenue touchpoints while maintaining a positive user experience.
